You earned it. Now give it a plan.

PayPortion is a simple zero-based budgeting app that tells you how to allocate each paycheck. Let's set it up in a minute.

1. Make it yours

2. What do you want to plan for? Tick everything that applies. You can add, rename or remove anything later.

3. Then

  1. Type the take-home pay you expect under Expected paychecks.
  2. Give every dollar a place until you reach $0 left. Type a dollar amount, or pick a % of your pay. Pick "Extra" in the % list on one line (like Savings) to send any extra, unplanned money there, so you always land on exactly $0.
  3. On payday, open 2. Payday (or swipe left) and drop in what you were paid. Tap 💵 Cash on anything you pay in cash.

Set this up once: what you expect to be paid, and where every dollar goes until you reach $0. Give a bill a due date and it's paid from the last paycheck before it's due. Tap 💵 Cash on anything you pay in cash, and payday tells you how much to withdraw.
